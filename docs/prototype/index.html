PROTOTYPE NOTES

This is the feel test, not the build. Open it on the Edge 40 and try to reach Instagram the way you would at 11pm.

Three tiers, three different costs

tier 0
phone, messages, camera, maps, clock — visible, one tap.
tier 1
whatsapp, gpay, gmail, uber, spotify… — invisible. Type 3+ characters, prefix match only, press enter.
tier 2
instagram, youtube, linkedin, reddit — invisible and unsearchable. Nothing appears until you type the name in full, exactly. Then the gate.

Why typing, not tapping

Seeing a coloured icon is recognition — automatic, pre-conscious. Typing a name is recall — it cannot happen without you deciding to do it. Nine keystrokes is enough time for the thumb to lose the argument.

What the strictness buys

For tier 2 there is no prefix match and no autocomplete. Typing instagra shows nothing at all — the screen stays empty right up to the last character. No partial reward, no near-miss.

The real app must set autocorrect="off" and autocapitalize="none", or Gboard will happily finish the word for you and hand back everything this mechanic just bought.

The gate is the product

Ten seconds of holding, with today's count in front of you the whole time. Release early and it resets. The reason you type is shown back to you on the way in — not stored, not judged, just quoted.

Note the asymmetry: not now is a wide, easy target. The way in is a small circle you have to keep your thumb on.

What this prototype can't show

Home → app is probably not how you actually get in. Notification → app and recents → app are, and no launcher can touch those. That needs an AccessibilityService watching TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED and throwing this same gate over the app whatever door you came through.
